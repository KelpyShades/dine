BACKGROUND & PROBLEM STATEMENT
==============================

WHAT PROBLEMS DID WE SOLVE?
---------------------------

1. HARD TO FIND FOOD OPTIONS
   - Customers had to call multiple restaurants or walk around to see what's available
   - No easy way to compare prices and menus from different places
   - Wasted time and effort just to place a food order

2. DIFFICULT FOR RESTAURANT OWNERS
   - Small restaurants struggled to reach more customers
   - No proper system to manage orders and track what's selling
   - Had to rely on phone calls and paper orders (messy and slow)

3. POOR COMMUNICATION
   - Customers didn't know if their order was ready
   - Restaurants couldn't easily update customers about delays
   - Led to confusion and unhappy customers

4. LIMITED ONLINE PRESENCE
   - Many small food vendors had no website or online menu
   - Customers couldn't see pictures of food or read descriptions
   - Hard for good restaurants to compete with bigger chains

HOW WE BUILT THE SOLUTION
-------------------------

TECHNOLOGY CHOICES (SIMPLE EXPLANATION):
- Flutter: Allows us to build one app that works on phones, tablets, and computers
- Supabase: Provides the "backend" - like the engine that stores data and makes everything work
- Real-time features: Orders update instantly, like WhatsApp messages

STEP-BY-STEP DEVELOPMENT:
1. First, we designed the app screens and user experience
2. Built the basic app structure with sample data to test ideas
3. Connected it to a real database to store actual information
4. Added features for both customers and restaurant owners
5. Made sure everything works smoothly across different devices
6. Added instant updates so everyone sees changes immediately

WHAT WE CREATED: THE DINE APP
----------------------------

Dine is a food ordering app that solves all these problems by providing:

FOR CUSTOMERS:
- One place to see menus from multiple restaurants
- Easy search to find exactly what you want to eat
- Simple ordering process with clear pricing
- Real-time updates on your order status

FOR RESTAURANT OWNERS:
- Professional online presence with photos and menus
- Easy-to-use dashboard to manage orders
- Analytics to see what's popular and track sales
- Instant notifications when new orders come in

FOR EVERYONE:
- Works on any device (phone, computer, tablet)
- Fast and reliable
- User-friendly design that anyone can understand

The result: Happy customers who can easily find and order food, and successful restaurant owners who can grow their business!
