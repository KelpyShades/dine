PROJECT GOALS & WHY THEY MATTER
===============================

MAIN GOALS OF THE DINE APP
--------------------------

1. MAKE FOOD ORDERING EASIER AND FASTER
   Goal: Replace slow phone calls and confusing processes with a simple app
   
   Why this matters: Studies show that digital ordering can make restaurants 20-25% more efficient. Since 2020, more people want to order food online (300% increase!).

2. CREATE ONE PLACE FOR MULTIPLE RESTAURANTS
   Goal: Build an app where many restaurants can sell their food together
   
   Why this matters: When restaurants join platforms like this, they can reach 40-60% more customers compared to being on their own. It's like having a digital food court!

3. INSTANT UPDATES FOR EVERYONE
   Goal: Make sure customers and restaurant owners get immediate updates about orders
   
   Why this matters: Research shows that when businesses use real-time updates, customers are 20-30% happier and operations become 35% more efficient.

4. MAKE THE APP EASY TO USE
   Goal: Design simple, beautiful screens that anyone can understand
   
   Why this matters: Good design can increase sales by up to 400%! When apps load faster (even by tiny amounts), more people complete their purchases.

5. BUILD SOMETHING THAT CAN GROW
   Goal: Create an app that won't break when more people start using it
   
   Why this matters: Well-built apps cost 40-50% less to maintain and can handle growth much better than poorly designed ones.

ADDITIONAL GOALS
---------------

6. HELP RESTAURANT OWNERS SUCCEED
   Goal: Give restaurant owners tools to track their sales and understand their customers
   
   Why this matters: Restaurants that use business analytics make 5-6% more profit and grow 15-20% faster.

7. WORK ON ALL DEVICES
   Goal: Make sure the app works perfectly whether you use a phone, tablet, or computer
   
   Why this matters: By 2024, most successful apps work on all devices. This approach costs 30-40% less to build and maintain.

8. MAKE FINDING FOOD SUPER EASY
   Goal: Create a smart search that helps people find exactly what they want to eat
   
   Why this matters: Good search features can increase sales by 15-25%. When search is bad, 68% of people give up and don't buy anything.

HOW WE APPROACHED THE PROJECT
----------------------------

OUR STRATEGY:
We followed proven methods for building successful apps:

- START SMALL, GROW BIG: We built a simple version first, then added more features based on what users actually wanted
- FOCUS ON USERS: Every decision was made by asking "Will this make it easier for customers and restaurant owners?"
- USE PROVEN TECHNOLOGY: We chose tools that other successful companies use
- TEST EVERYTHING: We made sure every feature works properly before adding the next one

WHAT WE EXPECT TO ACHIEVE
------------------------
- Orders process 40-50% faster than before
- Restaurants reach 25-30% more customers
- App works perfectly on 95%+ of all devices
- Updates happen instantly (faster than sending a text message)
- Can handle thousands of people using it at the same time
- 20-25% improvement in how happy users are

THE BOTTOM LINE
--------------
We built Dine using proven strategies and technologies that successful companies use. Our goals are realistic and based on real research about what works in the food and technology industries.
