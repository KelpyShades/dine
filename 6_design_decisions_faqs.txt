DESIGN DECISIONS AND FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
====================================================

This document explains the key design decisions made during the development of the Dine food ordering app and answers common questions about our approach.

PAYMENT AND FINANCIAL DECISIONS
===============================

Q: Why did you choose "Pay on Delivery" instead of online payments?
A: Simple - customers pay when they get their food. This way:
   - Customers trust the system more
   - No worry about losing money online
   - Local businesses like cash payments
   - Customers feel safer

Q: Doesn't this create problems with fake orders?
A: No, because vendors call customers to confirm orders first. This stops fake orders.

USER ACCOUNT AND REGISTRATION DECISIONS
=======================================

Q: Why don't customers need to create accounts?
A: To make ordering super easy and fast:
   - No passwords to remember
   - Order right away without signing up
   - People like quick ordering
   - We only ask for details when placing an order

Q: Why can't vendors register themselves? Why do they need admin to give them codes?
A: We want only good, real businesses on our platform:
   - Stops fake food sellers from joining
   - We check if businesses are real first
   - Protects customers from bad vendors
   - Keeps food quality high

COMMUNICATION AND NOTIFICATION DECISIONS
========================================

Q: Why do vendors call customers instead of sending emails or SMS?
A: Phone calls work better:
   - Emails might go to spam folder
   - Can talk about order details right away
   - Builds friendship between vendor and customer
   - Solves problems immediately
   - Everyone answers phone calls

Q: How do customers track their orders without automated tracking?
A: Vendors call customers with updates:
   - Vendors tell customers when food will be ready
   - Personal service is better than automated messages
   - Vendors give real timing, not fake estimates
   - Can explain any delays or changes

PLATFORM AND TECHNICAL DECISIONS
=================================

Q: Why didn't you develop for iOS? Why only Android and Web?
A: Android and Web reach more people faster:
   - Most people use Android phones
   - iPhone users can use the website
   - iOS apps cost more money to make
   - iOS apps take longer to approve
   - Website works on any phone

Q: Why use Flutter instead of native development?
A: Flutter lets us build one app that works everywhere:
   - Write code once, works on Android and Web
   - Faster and cheaper to build
   - App looks the same everywhere

BUSINESS MODEL DECISIONS
========================

Q: Why focus on local vendors instead of big restaurant chains?
A: We want to help small local businesses:
   - Small restaurants need more help than big chains
   - Supports our local community
   - Better personal service
   - Easier to work with local businesses

Q: How does the app make money without charging customers?
A: Vendors pay small fees:
   - Small commission from vendor sales
   - Vendors make more money, so they don't mind paying
   - Focus on helping vendors first

TECHNOLOGY AND COMPLEXITY DECISIONS
===================================

Q: Why use Supabase instead of building a custom backend?
A: Supabase saves time and money:
   - Ready-made database system
   - Real-time updates work automatically
   - Built-in security
   - Don't need to build everything from scratch

Q: Why keep the app simple instead of adding more features?
A: Simple apps work better:
   - Easy for vendors to learn
   - Fewer bugs and problems
   - Better user experience
   - Faster to build and fix

OPERATIONAL DECISIONS
====================

Q: What happens if vendors don't answer their phones?
A: We have backup plans:
   - Customers can call vendors directly
   - Try calling multiple times
   - Vendors know they must answer calls for business
   - We track which vendors don't answer

Q: How do you ensure food quality and safety?
A: We check vendors before they join:
   - Only approved businesses can sell food
   - Customer complaints help us find bad vendors
   - We talk directly with vendors about quality
   - Vendors must follow health department rules

Q: What if the app becomes very popular? Can it handle growth?
A: Yes, our system can grow:
   - Supabase handles many users automatically
   - Simple design is easier to expand
   - Vendors handle calls, not our servers
   - Website can serve many people

FUTURE CONSIDERATIONS
====================

Q: What features might you add in the future?
A: Based on what users want:
   - iPhone app if many people ask for it
   - Customer ratings for vendors
   - Simple order tracking messages
   - Better reports for vendors
   - Online payment options

Q: How would you handle customer complaints?
A: Multiple ways to solve problems:
   - Customers call vendors directly first
   - We step in for serious problems
   - Bad vendors get removed from platform
   - Personal talks solve most issues quickly

This FAQ explains why we made certain choices that might seem different from other food delivery apps.
